Looking for an air purifier? The right choice depends on the size of your room, the features you want, filter replacement costs, and how much you're comfortable spending.

What Should You Look for in an Air Purifier?

1. Room size

Choose a purifier rated for the room where you plan to use it. Pay attention to the air changes per hour (ACH) used for the manufacturer's room-size rating. A large square-footage claim based on one air change per hour is not directly comparable to a rating based on more frequent air changes.

2. Filter replacement costs

Filters need replacing according to the manufacturer's instructions. Compare the price and expected replacement schedule before choosing a model.

3. Noise level

If you plan to use your purifier in a bedroom, check its noise levels at the fan speeds you expect to use.

4. Features

Consider whether you want an air-quality sensor, automatic mode, smartphone controls, or a filter replacement indicator.

5. Clean Air Delivery Rate (CADR)

CADR measures how quickly an air purifier delivers filtered air. Compare CADR figures when choosing a model for your room.

Which Air Purifier Should You Choose?

  • Levoit Core 300S-P: Worth considering for a compact room and smart features.
  • Coway Airmega Mighty: Worth considering if you want its multi-stage filtration and washable pre-filter.
  • Blueair Blue Pure 311i Max: Worth considering if you want smart features and a model designed for a larger space.

Before purchasing, compare current prices, filter costs, room coverage, and warranty information directly with the manufacturer or retailer.
