Robot Vacuum Comparison: Roborock vs. MOVA

Want cleaner floors without spending hours vacuuming? Robot vacuums can help with everyday dust, crumbs, and pet hair. Here we compare three models at different price levels.

Last reviewed: November 2026

Quick Comparison

Robot Vacuum Best For Main Features
Roborock Q7 M5 Budget-conscious shoppers Mapping, scheduled cleaning, vacuuming and basic mopping
MOVA P10 Pro Ultra Automated vacuuming and mopping Self-emptying dock and automatic mop cleaning
MOVA P50 Pro Ultra More advanced cleaning Multifunction dock and advanced cleaning features

1. Roborock Q7 M5

Best for shoppers on a budget

The Roborock Q7 M5 is worth considering if you want everyday floor cleaning without paying for an expensive multifunction dock. It offers mapping and app controls that help schedule cleaning and organize rooms.

Advantages:

  • Designed for everyday cleaning.
  • Room mapping and app scheduling.
  • More affordable than many premium models.

Things to consider:

  • The standard model requires manual dustbin emptying.
  • Its basic mopping feature is not a replacement for regular mopping.
  • Check whether you are buying the Q7 M5 or Q7 M5+.

2. MOVA P10 Pro Ultra

Best for automated maintenance

The MOVA P10 Pro Ultra is designed for people who want more convenience. Its multifunction station can handle tasks such as dust emptying and mop-pad washing and drying.

Advantages:

  • Vacuuming and mopping features.
  • Automatic mop maintenance.
  • Less hands-on maintenance between cleaning sessions.

Things to consider:

  • It costs more than basic robot vacuums.
  • The dock needs additional floor space.
  • Check replacement mop pads and other accessory costs.

3. MOVA P50 Pro Ultra

Best for shoppers wanting more advanced cleaning

The MOVA P50 Pro Ultra is a higher-end option with a multifunction dock and features designed to make vacuuming and mopping more convenient. It is worth considering if you have different floor types and want more automated maintenance.

Advantages:

  • Advanced vacuuming and mopping features.
  • Automatic dock maintenance.
  • Designed for more demanding cleaning needs.

Things to consider:

  • Higher purchase price.
  • Requires space for its multifunction dock.
  • You may still need to pick up cords, toys, and clutter before cleaning.

What Should You Look For?

  • Flooring: Consider whether your home has carpet, hardwood, tile, or a mixture.
  • Pets: Look for brush designs that help reduce hair tangles.
  • Mapping: Room mapping makes scheduled cleaning easier.
  • Self-emptying: A cleaning dock can reduce daily maintenance.
  • Replacement parts: Check the cost of filters, brushes, and mop pads.

Our Takeaway

The Roborock Q7 M5 is worth comparing if keeping costs down is your priority. The MOVA P10 Pro Ultra offers more automated mop maintenance, while the MOVA P50 Pro Ultra is a higher-end option for shoppers wanting more advanced features.

Prices and product bundles can change. Always verify the exact model, included accessories, warranty, and current features before purchasing.
