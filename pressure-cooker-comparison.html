Instant Pot Comparison: Duo vs. Duo Plus vs. Pro

Looking for a pressure cooker that makes supper easier? Instant Pots can help prepare meals such as soups, beans, rice, meats, and one-pot dinners. Here are three popular models to consider.

Last reviewed: November 2026

Quick Comparison

Model Best For Main Features
Instant Pot Duo 6-Quart Everyday cooking and value Pressure cooking, slow cooking, rice, steaming and more
Instant Pot Duo Plus 6-Quart Extra cooking programs Additional presets, sous vide and sterilizing on supported versions
Instant Pot Pro 6-Quart More advanced home cooking Enhanced controls, cooking programs and a stovetop-friendly inner pot

1. Instant Pot Duo 6-Quart

Best for everyday cooking

The Instant Pot Duo is a popular starting point for people who want one appliance to handle several kitchen tasks. It can pressure cook, slow cook, steam, cook rice, sauté, and keep food warm.

Advantages:

  • Versatile for everyday meals.
  • Useful for soups, beans, meats, and rice.
  • Simple controls.
  • Good option for first-time pressure cooker owners.

Things to consider:

  • Fewer advanced features than some upgraded models.
  • Cooking capacity may be limited for very large families.
  • Always check which accessories are included.

2. Instant Pot Duo Plus 6-Quart

Best for additional cooking options

The Duo Plus builds on the basic Duo with additional cooking programs and convenience features. Depending on the version, it includes options such as sous vide and sterilizing.

Advantages:

  • More preset cooking options.
  • Useful for people who enjoy trying different recipes.
  • Versatile for weeknight meals and meal preparation.

Things to consider:

  • Extra programs may not be useful to everyone.
  • Usually costs more than the basic Duo.
  • Features can differ between product generations.

3. Instant Pot Pro 6-Quart

Best for more advanced home cooking

The Instant Pot Pro is designed for people who want additional controls and cooking convenience. Its features include customizable programs and an inner cooking pot designed for use on certain stovetops.

Advantages:

  • More cooking customization.
  • Multiple cooking functions.
  • Inner pot can be used on compatible stovetops.
  • Useful for people who regularly cook with an Instant Pot.

Things to consider:

  • Higher price than basic models.
  • Some features may be unnecessary for occasional users.
  • Check stovetop compatibility and care instructions.

What Size Pressure Cooker Should You Buy?

  • 3-Quart: Smaller households and side dishes.
  • 6-Quart: A versatile size for many families.
  • 8-Quart: Larger batches and bigger households.

What Should You Look For?

  • Capacity: Choose a size that suits your household.
  • Cooking programs: Consider which functions you will actually use.
  • Cleaning: Look for removable parts that are easy to wash.
  • Safety: Follow the manufacturer's pressure-cooking instructions.
  • Accessories: Compare included racks, lids, and other accessories.
  • Counter space: Measure your kitchen space before purchasing.

Our Takeaway

The Instant Pot Duo is a practical choice for everyday pressure cooking. The Duo Plus is worth considering if you want additional preset programs. The Pro is a good option for shoppers interested in more advanced controls and cooking features.

Before purchasing, compare the exact model number, capacity, included accessories, warranty, and current price. Features can vary by product generation.
