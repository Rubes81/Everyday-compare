Coffee Maker Buying Guide

Compare drip machines, single-serve brewers, carafes, programmability, and ongoing costs.

Updated November 2026 · Independent educational guide

## Choose your brewing routine A drip coffee maker is convenient for several cups at once. A single-serve machine can reduce waste when people want different drinks or brew at different times, but pods may cost more per serving and create extra packaging. ## Carafe choices Glass carafes usually sit on a warming plate. Thermal carafes are insulated and can keep coffee warm without continuous heating. Check replacement-carafe availability before choosing an unusual model. ## Useful features - Brew capacity that matches your household - Programmable start, if you will use it - Removable water reservoir for easier filling - A washable filter or standard paper-filter size - Simple descaling and cleaning instructions ## Think beyond the purchase price Include filters, pods, descaling products, and replacement parts in your budget. Look for clear cleaning instructions and a warranty you can understand. The best machine is the one that fits how much coffee you make and how much cleanup you are willing to do.
