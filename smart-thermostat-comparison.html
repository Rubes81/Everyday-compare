Smart Thermostats: Three Models Compared

A smart thermostat can help you manage your home's heating and cooling from an app, create schedules, and make temperature adjustments when you're away. But not every thermostat works with every heating and cooling system.

This guide compares the Google Nest Learning Thermostat, ecobee Smart Thermostat Premium, and Amazon Smart Thermostat. We'll look at their features and the things you should check before buying.

Quick Comparison

Features and compatibility vary by generation, system type, and configuration. Check the exact model before purchasing.

1. Google Nest Learning Thermostat

Best for: Homeowners who use Google Home and want a thermostat with learning features.

The Google Nest Learning Thermostat is designed to learn household temperature preferences and help create an automatic schedule. It can be controlled through the Google Home app and offers energy-saving features.

Highlights

  • Learning features that adapt to household routines.
  • Remote temperature control through the Google Home app.
  • Energy-saving scheduling features.
  • Smart home integration with compatible devices.
  • Modern display and design.

Things to Consider

  • Check compatibility with your HVAC system.
  • Some features depend on your home's wiring and setup.
  • Smart home features may vary by thermostat generation.
  • Review the exact model's installation requirements.

Official manufacturer information: Google Connected Home

2. ecobee Smart Thermostat Premium

Best for: Homeowners who want additional comfort features and a more advanced smart thermostat.

The ecobee Smart Thermostat Premium offers remote control, scheduling, smart home integration, and a built-in air quality monitor. It also includes smart speaker capabilities and can work with compatible ecobee SmartSensors.

Highlights

  • Built-in air quality monitoring features.
  • Smart home integration.
  • Remote control through the ecobee app.
  • Compatible SmartSensor support.
  • Built-in smart speaker features.

Things to Consider

  • Some additional features may require separate accessories.
  • Check whether your HVAC wiring is compatible.
  • Some services and integrations may require subscriptions.
  • It may offer more features than a basic thermostat shopper needs.

Official manufacturer information: ecobee Smart Thermostat Premium

3. Amazon Smart Thermostat

Best for: Alexa users who want straightforward smart temperature control.

The Amazon Smart Thermostat works with Alexa to provide app-based temperature control and scheduling. It is designed to integrate with compatible Alexa devices and routines.

Highlights

  • Alexa integration.
  • Remote temperature adjustments through the Alexa app.
  • Scheduling and smart home routines.
  • Designed for a straightforward smart thermostat experience.
  • Can work with compatible Alexa devices and services.

Things to Consider

  • Alexa is central to its smart home experience.
  • Check whether your HVAC system is supported.
  • Review wiring requirements before installation.
  • It has fewer built-in advanced features than some premium models.

Official manufacturer information: Amazon Smart Thermostat

Which Smart Thermostat Should You Choose?

  • Choose Google Nest if you use Google Home and like the idea of learning temperature preferences.
  • Consider ecobee Premium if you want additional comfort features, air quality monitoring, and sensor options.
  • Consider Amazon Smart Thermostat if you use Alexa and want a simpler connected thermostat.

Important: Check Compatibility Before Buying

A smart thermostat is not automatically compatible with every heating and cooling system. Before ordering, check:

  • Whether your system is a furnace, heat pump, boiler, or another type.
  • Whether you have a C-wire or need an approved power accessory.
  • Whether your system uses multiple heating or cooling stages.
  • Whether you have a dual-fuel or high-voltage system.
  • Whether your existing wiring matches the manufacturer's requirements.
  • Whether professional installation is recommended.

If you are unsure about your wiring, consult a qualified HVAC professional before installing a thermostat.

What to Look for Before Purchasing

  • Compatibility with your existing HVAC equipment.
  • Smart home platform support.
  • Ease of installation and setup.
  • Remote control and scheduling features.
  • Whether additional sensors are useful for your home.
  • Warranty, support, and return policy.
