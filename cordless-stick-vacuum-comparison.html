Best Cordless Stick Vacuums: Three Models Compared

Shopping for a cordless vacuum can be confusing. Some models focus on powerful cleaning features, while others emphasize convenience, hair pickup, or filtration. We compare three options to help you decide which features matter most for your home.

This guide looks at the Dyson V15 Detect, Shark Stratos Cordless, and Levoit LVAC-300.

Quick Comparison

Standout feature Dust illumination and particle detection Clean Sense IQ and DuoClean brushroll Anti-tangle brush and HEPA filtration

Specifications can vary by model number, product bundle, and included attachments. Always check the exact listing before purchasing.

1. Dyson V15 Detect

Best for: Shoppers who want advanced cleaning information and dust detection features.

The Dyson V15 Detect uses an illuminated cleaner head to make dust easier to see on hard floors. Its particle sensor measures debris passing through the vacuum, and its LCD screen displays cleaning information.

Highlights

  • Illuminated cleaner head for revealing dust on hard floors.
  • Particle detection and LCD cleaning information.
  • Up to 60 minutes of stated runtime under specified conditions.
  • Multiple attachments for different cleaning tasks.

Things to Consider

  • Typically positioned as a premium-priced vacuum.
  • Actual battery life changes with power mode and attachments.
  • Different bundles may include different cleaner heads and tools.

Official product information: Dyson V15 Detect

2. Shark Stratos Cordless (IZ862H)

Best for: Shoppers interested in automatic dirt detection and hair pickup features.

The Shark Stratos Cordless includes Clean Sense IQ, which detects dirt and automatically adjusts cleaning power. Its DuoClean PowerFins HairPro nozzle is designed to pick up hair and debris. It also includes Odor Neutralizer Technology.

Highlights

  • Clean Sense IQ detects dirt and adjusts power.
  • DuoClean PowerFins HairPro cleaning nozzle.
  • Odor Neutralizer Technology.
  • LED lights on the cleaning head.

Things to Consider

  • Heavier than the other two models in this comparison.
  • Features and included accessories depend on the exact package.
  • Check replacement filters and accessory availability before buying.

Official product information: Shark Stratos Cordless

3. Levoit LVAC-300

Best for: Shoppers interested in a lighter design, hard-floor cleaning, and anti-tangle features.

The Levoit LVAC-300 features TripleStrike anti-tangle technology, five-stage HEPA filtration, and a detachable battery. Levoit advertises up to 60 minutes of runtime in Eco mode using the crevice tool. Runtime will be shorter with other attachments and higher power settings.

Highlights

  • TripleStrike anti-tangle brush technology.
  • Five-stage HEPA filtration.
  • Detachable battery and dust bin.
  • LED lights on the vacuum head.
  • Up to 60 minutes of stated Eco-mode runtime with the crevice tool.

Things to Consider

  • Levoit describes the vacuum as especially suited to hard floors.
  • Turbo mode has a much shorter stated runtime.
  • Consider your carpet type and cleaning needs before purchasing.

Official product information: Levoit LVAC-300

Which Cordless Vacuum Should You Choose?

  • Choose the Dyson V15 Detect if dust detection, cleaning information, and multiple attachments are priorities.
  • Consider the Shark Stratos if you want dirt-sensing features and a brushroll designed for hair pickup.
  • Consider the Levoit LVAC-300 if you value its anti-tangle design, detachable battery, and filtration features.

What to Check Before Buying

  • Whether your home has mostly carpet, rugs, or hard floors.
  • How long the vacuum runs with the floor head attached.
  • Whether the battery can be replaced.
  • Which attachments are included in the package.
  • The cost and availability of replacement filters and parts.
  • The manufacturer's warranty and return policy.
