Robot Lawn Mowers: Three Models Compared

Want to spend less time mowing your lawn? A robotic lawn mower can help maintain your grass on a schedule. Different models use different navigation systems, and the best choice depends on your lawn size, slopes, layout, and budget.

This guide compares the Husqvarna Automower 115H, Segway Navimow i105N, and Mammotion LUBA mini AWD 800.

Quick Comparison

Feature Husqvarna 115H Segway Navimow i105N Mammotion LUBA mini AWD 800

Lawn-area recommendations and features depend on the exact model and region. Verify the model number and manufacturer's specifications before purchasing.

1. Husqvarna Automower 115H

Best for: Homeowners who prefer a traditional boundary-wire robotic mower.

The Husqvarna Automower 115H uses boundary and guide wires to define its mowing area and help it return to its charging station. It is designed for regular lawn maintenance and can handle slopes within the manufacturer's stated limits.

Highlights

  • Boundary-wire installation defines where the mower can travel.
  • Guide wire helps it return to the charging station.
  • Designed for lawns up to approximately 0.4 acre.
  • Small pivoting blades trim grass gradually.
  • App and onboard controls are available.

Things to Consider

  • Boundary-wire installation takes planning and setup.
  • Damaged or disconnected wire may interrupt mowing.
  • Check slope limits and lawn layout before purchasing.

Official manufacturer information: Husqvarna Automower 115H

2. Segway Navimow i105N

Best for: Homeowners interested in wire-free lawn mapping and app-based mowing schedules.

The Segway Navimow i105N uses positioning technology and AI-assisted mapping to establish virtual mowing boundaries. Its app allows users to manage mowing areas and schedules. VisionFence technology helps detect obstacles.

Highlights

  • Wire-free virtual boundary setup.
  • EFLS positioning technology.
  • AI-assisted mapping and obstacle avoidance.
  • App control and multi-zone management.
  • Recommended mowing area of approximately 1/8 acre.

Things to Consider

  • Satellite and positioning conditions can affect performance.
  • Trees, buildings, and other obstructions may affect navigation.
  • Check the recommended lawn area against your actual grass area.

Official manufacturer information: Segway Navimow i105

3. Mammotion LUBA mini AWD 800

Best for: Homeowners with sloped or uneven yards who want an all-wheel-drive robotic mower.

The Mammotion LUBA mini AWD 800 combines all-wheel drive with RTK positioning and AI vision. It uses virtual mapping to manage mowing areas and is designed to handle challenging terrain.

Highlights

  • All-wheel-drive system.
  • Manufacturer-stated climbing ability up to 80% under specified conditions.
  • Virtual mapping and multiple mowing zones.
  • AI vision for positioning and obstacle detection.
  • Recommended mowing area of approximately 0.2 acre for the 800 model.

Things to Consider

  • Initial mapping and setup require attention.
  • Positioning conditions and obstacles can affect navigation.
  • Confirm whether the 800 model is large enough for your lawn.
  • Check what positioning equipment and accessories are included.

Official manufacturer information: Mammotion LUBA mini AWD

Which Robot Lawn Mower Should You Choose?

  • Choose the Husqvarna 115H if you are comfortable installing boundary wire and want a wire-guided system.
  • Consider the Segway Navimow i105N if you want virtual boundaries and app-based mowing management for a smaller yard.
  • Consider the Mammotion LUBA mini AWD 800 if your yard has slopes or uneven terrain and you want all-wheel drive.

What to Check Before Buying

  • Measure the actual grass area, not the entire property.
  • Check the steepest slopes and narrow passages in your yard.
  • Consider trees, buildings, fences, and satellite signal conditions.
  • Find out whether boundary wires or positioning equipment are needed.
  • Check how the mower handles separate front and back lawn areas.
  • Review blade replacement costs and maintenance requirements.
  • Read the warranty, return policy, and safety instructions.
