Ninja vs NutriBullet: Which Full-Size Blender Is Better?

Shopping for a full-size blender? Ninja and NutriBullet both offer countertop models designed for smoothies, sauces, frozen drinks and everyday kitchen prep.

In this guide, we compare the Ninja Professional Plus BN701 and the NutriBullet SmartSense Blender Combo.

Important: Everyday Compare has not personally tested these products. This guide uses published manufacturer specifications and attributed independent research.

Quick Comparison

Feature Ninja BN701 NutriBullet SmartSense
Blender type Full-size Full-size
Pitcher capacity 72 oz total 64 oz
Motor power 1200 watts 1400 watts
Controls Auto-iQ programs 5 speeds and smart auto cycle
Best feature Ice crushing Automatic blending adjustments

1. Ninja Professional Plus Blender BN701

The Ninja Professional Plus is designed for people who want a powerful countertop blender for family-sized recipes.

Key Features

  • 72-ounce pitcher with 64-ounce maximum liquid capacity
  • 1200-watt motor
  • Total Crushing blade assembly
  • Three Auto-iQ programs
  • Dishwasher-safe pitcher and blade assembly

Potential Advantages

  • Large pitcher for family portions
  • Designed to crush ice for frozen drinks
  • Automatic blending programs

Things to Consider

  • Check the height and counter space needed.
  • Its stacked blade assembly needs careful handling.
  • It is not designed for hot blending.

2. NutriBullet SmartSense Blender Combo

The NutriBullet SmartSense offers a full-size pitcher, multiple speed settings and an automatic blending cycle that adjusts to the ingredients.

Key Features

  • 64-ounce pitcher
  • 1400-watt motor
  • Five variable speeds
  • Pulse function
  • Smart automatic blending cycle
  • Includes a tamper

Potential Advantages

  • Automatic adjustments while blending
  • Multiple manual speed choices
  • Large pitcher for smoothies and family recipes

Things to Consider

  • Its pitcher is smaller than the Ninja's.
  • Check the included accessories for the exact package.
  • Review the manual for safe hot-liquid use.

View NutriBullet SmartSense details

Which Blender Should You Choose?

Consider Ninja if:

  • You frequently make frozen drinks.
  • You want a larger pitcher.
  • You like preset blending programs.

Consider NutriBullet if:

  • You want multiple manual speed settings.
  • You like automatic blending adjustments.
  • You want a slightly smaller pitcher.

What to Look for Before Buying

  • Pitcher capacity and maximum liquid fill
  • Motor power and blending controls
  • Ease of cleaning
  • Countertop dimensions
  • Warranty and replacement parts
  • Current price and return policy

Our Final Takeaway

Both blenders offer useful features for everyday kitchen tasks. Ninja emphasizes ice crushing and a larger pitcher, while NutriBullet offers five speeds and a smart automatic blending cycle.

The best choice depends on what you prepare most often, how much space you have and which features matter to you. Compare the exact model and package before purchasing.
