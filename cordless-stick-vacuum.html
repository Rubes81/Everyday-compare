Choosing a Cordless Stick Vacuum

A checklist for runtime, floor types, weight, filtration, dust bins, attachments, and replacement batteries.

Updated November 2026 · Independent educational guide

## Start with your floors If your home is mostly hard flooring, a soft roller or a head designed for hard floors may be useful. For carpet, look for a powered brush roll and check how the manufacturer describes performance on your carpet type. Homes with a mix of surfaces need a head that transitions well. ## Runtime is not one number Advertised runtime is often measured in an economy mode with a non-motorized tool. Powered heads and higher suction settings can shorten battery life. Check whether the battery is removable and whether replacements are available. ## Everyday usability - Total weight and balance in your hand - Bin capacity and how easily it empties - Hair-wrap management and brush-roll access - Included crevice and upholstery tools - Filter washing and replacement schedule ## Make a realistic choice Consider the size of your home and whether you need to clean stairs, upholstery, or a car. A lighter vacuum you can grab easily may be more useful than a heavier model with features you rarely need.
