Air Fryer Comparison: COSORI TurboBlaze vs. Ninja AF141 vs. Ninja DualZone Pro

The right air fryer depends on your portions, counter space, and whether you want one large basket or two separate cooking zones.

Research checked November 8, 2026. Everyday Compare has not personally tested these appliances.

Quick take: Consider the COSORI TurboBlaze for a roomy single basket and multiple cooking modes; the Ninja AF141 for a simpler 5-quart single basket; and a Ninja DualZone model if cooking two foods separately at once is your priority.

1. COSORI TurboBlaze 6-quart

COSORI advertises a 6-quart single-basket air fryer with multiple cooking functions and a temperature range up to 450°F. RTINGS measured its tested unit at 6.3 quarts and 75.5 square inches of tray surface area.

  • Consider it if: you want a larger single basket and flexibility beyond basic air frying.
  • Check: exact model code, basket dimensions, coating and care instructions.
  • Trade-off: one basket cannot cook two foods at independently controlled settings simultaneously.

View COSORI TurboBlaze at COSORI.

2. Ninja Air Fryer Pro AF141

The AF141 is a 5-quart single-basket model with four advertised functions: air fry, roast, reheat, and dehydrate. Ninja lists a maximum temperature of 400°F. RTINGS measured its tested unit at 5.1 quarts and 64.9 square inches of tray surface area.

  • Consider it if: you want a straightforward single-basket design and usually cook smaller batches.
  • Check: whether the 5-quart basket suits your portions and whether the listing includes the accessories you expect.
  • Trade-off: smaller measured tray area than the tested TurboBlaze may mean cooking larger portions in batches.

View Ninja AF141 at Ninja.

3. Ninja DualZone Pro

DualZone models use two independent baskets so you can prepare separate foods with different settings. A retailer listing for the DZ800 variant describes an 8-quart, two-basket design. Ninja has several DualZone models, so verify the exact model number, capacity, and controls.

  • Consider it if: you regularly cook a main dish and side at the same time.
  • Check: each basket's capacity, counter width and depth, drawer size, and sync/finish features.
  • Trade-off: two baskets and a wider body take more counter and storage space.

View the Ninja DualZone Pro DZ800 retailer listing. Confirm seller and model details before buying.

What independent testing says

RTINGS' published comparison of the COSORI TurboBlaze and Ninja AF141 reports larger measured basket surface area for the TurboBlaze (75.5 vs. 64.9 square inches) and a larger measured chamber (6.3 vs. 5.1 quarts). In that test, RTINGS found the TurboBlaze produced more evenly cooked results overall, while the Ninja AF141 preheated and cooked faster in some tests and held a more stable temperature.

These are RTINGS results, not tests performed by Everyday Compare. Results vary with batch size, food placement, recipe, and exact product revision. The DualZone model is included for its different two-basket layout, not as a claim that it won the same lab comparison.

How to choose

  • One or two people: a compact single basket may be enough for modest portions.
  • Three or more people: compare basket floor area and single-layer space, not only quart labels.
  • Two foods at once: a dual-basket model can help when foods need different settings.
  • Limited counter space: measure width, depth, height, and ventilation clearance.

Questions before buying

  1. How many servings do I cook at once?
  2. Do I want one basket or two separate zones?
  3. Will it fit on my counter with ventilation clearance?
  4. Are the basket and crisper plate easy to clean?
  5. What are the warranty, return, and replacement-part terms?
  6. Does the exact model number match the listing specifications?

Our summary

Put the COSORI TurboBlaze on your shortlist if you want a larger single-basket design. Compare the Ninja AF141 if you prefer a simpler 5-quart basket. Look at a Ninja DualZone model if cooking two foods separately is most important. There is no universal winner: your portions, space, and routine should decide.
