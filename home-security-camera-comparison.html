We compare three wireless outdoor cameras from eufy, Google, and Ring. We'll look at video quality, power, storage, subscriptions, and features.

1. eufy SoloCam S340

Local storage and solar-powered convenience

The eufy SoloCam S340 features two cameras, 3K wide-angle video, a 2K telephoto camera, and pan-and-tilt coverage. It includes built-in 8 GB storage and an adjustable solar panel.

Things to like

  • Dual-camera design
  • 360-degree pan-and-tilt coverage
  • Built-in local storage
  • No required monthly subscription for basic local storage
  • Solar panel included

Things to consider

  • Needs adequate sunlight for solar charging.
  • Uses 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi.
  • Motion-triggered recording rather than continuous recording on battery.
  • Check compatibility before adding a HomeBase.

Manufacturer information: eufy SoloCam S340

2. Google Nest Cam (Battery)

Smart monitoring with Google Home

The Google Nest Cam Battery offers 1080p HDR video, night vision, two-way audio, and a rechargeable battery. It works through the Google Home app.

Things to like

  • 1080p HDR video
  • 130-degree diagonal field of view
  • Two-way audio
  • Person, animal, and vehicle detection features
  • Indoor or outdoor placement

Things to consider

  • Requires Wi-Fi and the Google Home app.
  • Includes up to three hours of event video history without a subscription.
  • Longer video history requires a paid plan.
  • Battery operation does not support continuous 24/7 video history.

3. Ring Outdoor Cam Battery

Flexible placement and Ring app features

Ring Outdoor Cam is a battery-powered camera designed for flexible indoor or outdoor placement. It offers 1080p HD video, color night vision, motion alerts, and two-way talk.

Things to like

What Should You Look for in a Security Camera?

1. Video quality

Look at resolution, field of view, and night vision. Higher resolution can help reveal details, but camera placement and lighting also matter.

2. Storage and monthly fees

Some cameras store recordings locally, while others rely on cloud subscriptions. Compare what is included for free and what requires a paid plan.

3. Battery, solar, or wired power

Battery cameras offer flexible installation. Solar accessories may reduce charging, while wired cameras can provide more consistent power.

4. Motion detection

Look for customizable motion zones and alerts that help reduce unnecessary notifications.

5. Privacy and security

Check account security, privacy settings, camera access controls, and how video recordings are stored.

Which Camera Should You Consider?

  • eufy SoloCam S340: Worth considering if local storage, solar charging, and flexible camera movement are important.
  • Google Nest Cam Battery: Worth considering if you already use Google Home and want smart alerts.
  • Ring Outdoor Cam: Worth considering if you use Ring products and want flexible battery-powered installation.

Before purchasing, compare current prices, subscription terms, storage options, and warranty information. Features can change over time.
